# Cite and acknowledge RCC in your paper

_Publishing RCC-enabled work_

When RCC compute, project storage, scientific software, transfer services, or operational support materially contributed to a scholarly publication, authors must acknowledge RCC in the Methods or Acknowledgements section and must cite EMCP, RCC's canonical published infrastructure paper, in the bibliography. Both acknowledgement and citation are required.

Suggested acknowledgement: “Computational resources and services were provided by the Research Compute Cluster (RCC) at University Hospital Essen.”

Required infrastructure citation (EMCP): Schmidt, Hendrik F. R.; Schlötterer, Jörg; Bargull, Marcel; Nasca, Enrico; Aydelott, Ryan; Seifert, Christin; Meyer, Folker. Towards a Trustworthy, Secure and Reliable Enclave for Machine Learning in a Hospital Setting: The Essen Medical Computing Platform (EMCP). 2021 IEEE Third International Conference on Cognitive Machine Intelligence (CogMI), pp. 116–123. DOI: [10.1109/CogMI52975.2021.00023](https://doi.org/10.1109/cogmi52975.2021.00023).

EMCP is the published architectural precursor to RCC and remains the canonical infrastructure citation until RCC formally designates a dedicated published RCC methods or infrastructure paper.

- **Acknowledge** — Use the stable name “Research Compute Cluster (RCC)” so the contribution is discoverable.

- **Cite** — Cite EMCP, the canonical published RCC infrastructure lineage paper, in the bibliography.

- **Identify** — Keep the DOI or PMID together with the RCC project name after publication.

- **Report** — Provide the DOI or PMID through your normal RCC project contact so the publication can be attributed to the right project.

- **Attribute** — RCC reviews project-attributed publication records before they enter institutional impact reporting.
