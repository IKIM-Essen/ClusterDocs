# Publications and acknowledging RCC

Research publications are one of the clearest ways to show what RCC enables. If RCC compute, project storage, scientific software, data-transfer services, or operational support materially contributed to your work, please make that contribution visible in the paper.

## Recommended acknowledgement

Use this wording in the **Methods** or **Acknowledgements** section:

> Computational resources and services were provided by the Research Compute Cluster (RCC) at University Hospital Essen.

Please keep the stable name **Research Compute Cluster (RCC)** in the text. Consistent wording makes RCC-supported publications easier to identify without relying on author names alone.

RCC does not currently designate a canonical methods or infrastructure paper for citation. Until one is formally designated, use the acknowledgement above rather than citing an unpublished manuscript.

## Current RCC publication archive

The RCC website carries the current reviewed list under [Publications](https://rcc.ikim.uk-essen.de/#rcc-publications).

The initial historical seed contains **32 Folker Meyer scholarly outputs from 2020 through 2026**. The seed uses the exact Folker Meyer scholarly identity for discovery and was explicitly approved as a bounded historical import. Thirty entries are published; two very recent August 2026 Frontiers papers already have DOI records but are labelled **accepted** until final publication. Correction notices stay attached to the parent paper rather than being counted as additional publications.

This seed is not a rule that every paper by an RCC user automatically belongs in the RCC archive. New publications follow the ordinary project-attribution and review workflow below.

## After your paper is published

Keep these two pieces of information together:

- the **DOI or PMID** of the publication;
- the **RCC project name** that supported the work.

Then provide them through your normal RCC project contact. This lets the RCC team attribute the publication to the correct project and include it in reviewed institutional impact reporting.

If several RCC projects or facilities contributed, add a short note describing that relationship rather than choosing one project arbitrarily.

## Why RCC asks for this

Publication reporting is intended to form a closed loop:

1. researchers acknowledge RCC;
2. the DOI/PMID becomes a publication candidate;
3. the candidate is associated with the relevant RCC project;
4. the attribution is reviewed;
5. only an accepted record enters RCC impact and reporting views.

Automated literature discovery may help find publications that users forget to report, but search results are **candidates only**. They are not accepted automatically. An RCC account appearing in an author list is not enough evidence by itself that RCC contributed to that paper.

This model prevents the reporting system from guessing project ownership later and makes annual reports, funding evidence, project summaries, and institutional statistics traceable back to a reviewed publication record.

## What if I used another core facility too?

Acknowledge each contributing facility according to its own policy. For example, an IMCES-assisted study may need both the RCC acknowledgement and the appropriate IMCES/instrument funding acknowledgement. These describe different contributions and should not be collapsed into one generic infrastructure sentence.

## What changes later?

A future My RCC publication-reporting workflow may let users submit DOI/PMID and project attribution directly. Until that workflow is implemented and enabled, this documentation deliberately does not claim that a publication-submission form exists.
